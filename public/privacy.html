Privacy & Data Protection Policy

Your health data privacy is our highest priority. Heal in India adheres to modern security-by-design standards aligned with HIPAA, Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDPA), and global health confidentiality benchmarks.

1. Health Records Ownership

You own 100% of your uploaded diagnostic reports, prescriptions, and Ayurvedic assessments. You maintain granular control over which clinic or doctor has permission to access your files.

2. Data Encryption

All data in transit is protected using TLS 1.3 encryption, and data at rest is secured using AES-256 standards.

3. No Sale of Health Data

We never sell, rent, or monetize your health information, search queries, or medical tourism itineraries to third-party advertisers.
