Understanding Classical Panchakarma

Panchakarma (literally "five actions" in Sanskrit) is the cornerstone of Ayurvedic restorative medicine. It is not merely a relaxation massage, but a clinical metabolic and cellular purification protocol that removes accumulated toxins (Ama) and balances the Tridoshas (Vata, Pitta, Kapha).

1. Purvakarma
Preparation Phase

Internal and external oleation (Snehana) with warm medicated ghee and herbal steam (Swedana) to loosen deep-seated toxins.

2. Pradhanakarma
Main Cleansing

The core therapeutic elimination procedures tailored to your Prakriti: Vamana, Virechana, Basti, Nasyam, or Raktamokshana.

3. Paschatkarma
Post-Care & Rasayana

Gradual reintroduction of diet (Samsarjana Krama), cell-nourishing rasayanas, yoga, and lifestyle maintenance guidelines.
