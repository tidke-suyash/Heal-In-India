Medical Disclaimer

Heal in India is an integrated digital platform that connects patients and medical tourists with verified healthcare facilities, Ayurvedic hospitals, Panchakarma centers, and certified practitioners across India.

Important Notice: Heal in India does not provide medical diagnosis, treatment prescriptions, or direct medical advice. Content on this platform is for informational and booking coordination purposes only.

1. Not a Substitute for Professional Medical Advice

Always seek the direct guidance of a licensed physician, registered Vaidya, or qualified healthcare provider with any questions you may have regarding a medical condition.

2. Emergency Situations

If you are experiencing a medical emergency, call 112 (in India), your local emergency services, or proceed to the nearest hospital emergency room immediately.

3. Visa & Government Disclaimers

Heal in India assists international patients with clinic invitation documentation. Visa decisions are made exclusively by the Ministry of Home Affairs and Foreigners Regional Registration Offices (FRRO), Government of India.
