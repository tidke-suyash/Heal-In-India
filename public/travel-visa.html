Frequently Asked Questions by Medical Tourists

What is the Ayush Visa (AY-1)?

The Ayush Visa is a specialized e-Visa created specifically for foreigners visiting India for medical treatments under traditional Indian systems of medicine (Ayurveda, Yoga, Panchakarma, Naturopathy, etc.). It allows multiple entries and up to 6 months of stay.

Can an attendant or family member travel with me?

Yes. Up to two family members or medical attendants can travel under the Ayush Attendant Visa (AY-2) which has the same validity as the primary patient's visa.

How do I get airport transfers and translation?

Our partner clinics feature dedicated international desks. When booking on Heal in India, airport pickup from Cochin, Delhi, Mumbai, or Goa can be included with English and multilingual staff.

Are clinical health records safe?

Yes. You retain total ownership of your medical documents. You can upload past test results securely to your Heal in India portal and selectively grant access to your attending Vaidya.
